How to Attract Northern Cardinals to Your Yard: 5 Proven Steps

Published: August 16, 2026 Category: Species Guides Reading Time: 5 min

The Northern Cardinal (*Cardinalis cardinalis*) is one of the most recognizable and beloved backyard songbirds in North America. With their striking scarlet plumage, distinct crest, and rich whistling songs, attracting a resident pair of cardinals brings color and activity to any outdoor space.

1. Provide Their Preferred High-Oil Seeds

Northern Cardinals possess heavy, conical beaks built for cracking tough seed shells. Their top dietary preferences include:

  • Black-Oil Sunflower Seeds: The absolute favorite food for cardinals due to high fat and protein content.
  • Safflower Seeds: Highly favored by cardinals, but widely disliked by squirrels and nuisance blackbirds.
  • Shelled Peanuts: Excellent winter energy supplement.
Pro Tip: Cardinals prefer feeding at dawn and dusk. Ensure feeders are replenished before early morning foraging begins.

2. Choose the Right Feeder Design

Unlike clinging chickadees or finches, cardinals need sturdy, flat perches. Use broad platform (tray) feeders, heavy wooden hopper feeders, or large ground feeding zones with adequate predator clearance.
